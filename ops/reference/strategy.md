# Content strategy

**Clip whatever is most popular — any topic.** Discovery already ranks the
channel's VODs by view count, so the queue is popularity-ordered. Just work it
top-down.

- Source channel: `@TheSaintandTheSinner` (Marquett Burton). Longform debates,
  interviews, reactions, confrontations. The biggest hits span dating, race,
  debates, geopolitics — don't restrict to one theme.
- Titles come out **point-based** (state the claim/hook, no speaker names). This
  is intentional — the transcript has no speaker labels, so naming who said what
  risks fabricating attribution. Do **not** "fix" titles to add names.
- **Cut off-brand clips** before posting: anything with slurs, explicit
  white-nationalist framing, or that's pure meta (just debate-scheduling or
  "you got owned" with no substance). When unsure, skip it.
- Length sweet spot 35–90s; over ~110s hurts completion but isn't banned.
